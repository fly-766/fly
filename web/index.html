What still requires trust ＋

Neural computation runs off-chain. Source, inputs, and outputs can be replayed. A fingerprint on chain can detect later edits; it cannot prove the step ran. Fills and funding depend on the venue and a settlement signer; contracts re-check inventory. Bridges depend on Circle. Vault rules depend on IGNIX.

An operator can pause or stop. v0.4 recovery has no extra waiting period once flat and confirmed; that is not a holder redeem right. IGNIX creator extras still accrue to an EOA.

Trading can lose principal. Caps are not a return. The official token is published with the site, not from leftover trials.
